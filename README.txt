MOOSE MERCH WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js

How to use:
1. Upload all three files to your web host.
2. Create PayPal Payment Links or Buy Buttons in your PayPal Business account.
3. Open index.html and replace these placeholders:
   https://www.paypal.com/ncp/payment/YOUR-HOODIE-LINK
   https://www.paypal.com/ncp/payment/YOUR-TEE-LINK
   https://www.paypal.com/ncp/payment/YOUR-STICKER-LINK
4. Replace product names, prices, and descriptions.
5. Replace the mock product blocks with real product images if you want.

Important:
This is the simple redirect-to-PayPal version. It avoids storing payment info on your site.
